BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)

The number of calories your body burns at complete rest. Uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation — the most accurate formula for the general population.

BMR drops as you lose weight and as you age — which is exactly why crash diets fail. Preserving muscle through adequate protein and strength training keeps BMR high.

TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)

Your total daily calorie burn — BMR plus everything you do. This is the number that tells you what to eat to maintain, lose or gain weight.

MAP (Mean Arterial Pressure)

The average pressure in your arteries during one cardiac cycle. Critical for organ perfusion — used in ICU care and for anyone on BP medication.

MAP is calculated as (SBP + 2 × DBP) ÷ 3. A MAP of at least 60 mmHg is required to perfuse vital organs. Below that, tissue ischemia can occur.
